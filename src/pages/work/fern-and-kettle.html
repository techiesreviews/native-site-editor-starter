Cafe · Identity and site · 2025

Fern & Kettle

A one-page site with a menu the owners change themselves before opening each morning.

The brief

Fern & Kettle opened with a chalkboard menu and a social media account that nobody had time to update. Customers kept asking whether the cafe did breakfast, which it did, and whether it took bookings, which it did too.

What we built

A single page with the opening hours at the top, the menu below, and a booking link that goes straight to the cafe's existing calendar. The menu prints cleanly on one sheet, so the counter copy and the website are always the same.

Since launch

The owners update the menu most mornings in a couple of minutes. Phone calls asking about opening hours have mostly stopped.
