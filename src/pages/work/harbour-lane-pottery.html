Harbour Lane Pottery

A quiet portfolio for a working potter, with large photographs and a dates page for kiln openings.

The brief

The studio sold most of its work at kiln openings, but news of the dates travelled by word of mouth and a mailing list kept in a notebook. The potter wanted somewhere calm to show finished pieces without running an online shop.

What we built

A portfolio of large photographs with short captions, grouped by glaze, and a dates page that lists the next openings with directions to the studio. Adding a date is one line of text.

Since launch

Openings are now announced a month ahead, and the potter adds new pieces after each firing without asking us.
