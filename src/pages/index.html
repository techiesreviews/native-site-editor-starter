Larkspur is a two-person design studio for small, useful websites.

We work with independent shops, studios, and community groups that need a clear site and a calm way to keep it up to date. Everything we build is plain HTML and CSS you can read.

Selected work

Fern & Kettle Cafe · Identity and site · 2025

A neighbourhood cafe needed a menu that the owners could change themselves before opening each morning. We designed a one-page site with a printable menu and a simple booking link.

 Harbour Lane Pottery Ceramics studio · Portfolio · 2025

A working potter wanted a quiet place to show finished pieces and announce kiln openings. Large photographs, short captions, and a dates page that takes minutes to update.

 Meadow Row Allotments Community group · Notices and rules · 2024

Forty plot holders share one noticeboard. We turned a pile of photocopied rules into a small site with a plot map, seasonal notices, and a contact page for the committee.
