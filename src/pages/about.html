About Larkspur

Larkspur is run by two people, a designer and a front-end developer, from a shared studio above a bookshop in Frome, Somerset. We have worked together since 2019.

We take on a handful of projects each year so that each one gets real attention. Most of our clients are small businesses and local organisations around Somerset who have been putting off their website for a while.

We prefer plain HTML and CSS over frameworks, system fonts over webfonts, and a few well-chosen photographs over stock imagery. Sites load quickly and keep working for years without maintenance.

Every site is handed over with a short written guide and a walkthrough, so that the people who own it can also look after it.
